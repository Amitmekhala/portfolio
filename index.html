About me

A bit about me

I'm Amit, a web developer from Udhampur in Jammu & Kashmir. I started out in 2022 at iWizardz, building WordPress and Elementor websites for small businesses, and learned early that a good-looking page is only half the job.

At Growthify Media I spent my days on the other half: landing pages for workshops and webinars, and the HubSpot, GoHighLevel, Zapier and Make workflows behind them, so that every lead got a reminder and a follow-up without anyone doing it by hand.

Today I work as a Senior Web Developer at Perfomity Media, mostly inside Shopify themes: product pages, offer blocks, cart drawers and the small details that decide whether someone clicks Buy Now. I like to work carefully inside a live store, keeping the existing apps and integrations working while I make changes.

If you have a store, a funnel or a messy lead process, I'd be happy to take a look.

— Amit
